Vero Compass rotates a pool of major crypto assets by momentum, sizes positions by risk-parity, and steps aside to cash when Bitcoin breaks its long-term trend. The rules are the product; the coin pool is a disclosed input you can change.

Credibility doesn't come from a screenshot. It comes from a record that accumulates continuously, in public, in real time, on infrastructure we own — witnessed forming, not posted after the fact. This page is that record.
